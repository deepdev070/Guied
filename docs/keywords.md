## 1. Keyword Architecture & Intent Classification

Queries are grouped across 6 distinct citizen search intents:

1. **`documents_required`**: Citizens seeking the exact checklist of proofs needed before heading to an office or portal.
2. **`fees`**: Citizens wanting to know the honest, statutory government fee to avoid agent overcharging.
3. **`status`**: Citizens checking application progress, tracking speed post dispatch, or verifying approvals.
4. **`online_apply`**: Citizens wanting direct `.gov.in` portal links and step-by-step digital application flows.
5. **`rejection`**: Citizens researching why their application was rejected, put on hold, or failed verification.
6. **`how_long`**: Citizens asking about turnaround time, statutory delivery guarantees (RTS Act), and validity periods.
